A budget is a spending limit in dollars. It can sit on a **person**, a **team**
or the **organization**, and over a **day**, a **week** or a **month**. That is
up to nine limits reaching one person, and any of them can be the one that
stops them.

Each limit has two lines:

- **Soft limit**: the warning line. Requests are still served, except on
  [routes you marked as stopping there](/docs/configure/routes/#decide-what-happens-past-a-soft-limit).
  The engineer is told which routes still work.
- **Hard limit**: the stop line. Every request that budget governs is refused
  until the window resets or somebody raises the limit.

Limits are set on **People & teams**. Routes react to budgets and cannot set
one.

## Set a limit on a team

1. Open **People & teams → Teams** and click the team.
2. Under **Limits**, pick the window: **Daily**, **Weekly** or **Monthly**. The
   panel opens on Monthly. These are three separate budgets, so check which tab
   you are on before typing a figure.
3. Switch **Hard limit** to **On** and type an amount. Do the same for **Soft
   limit** if you want a warning line before it.
4. Look at the grid beside the panel (see the next section), then press **Save
   changes**.

It is in force from the next request. A tab with a limit set shows a mark, so
you can see at a glance which windows are capped.

**For a person**, open **People & teams → People** and click them. The panel is
the same.

**For the whole organization**, open **People & teams → Teams** and click the
first card, **This organization**.

To remove a limit, switch both lines **Off** and save.

## Read what actually stops somebody

Beside every limit panel is a grid titled **Every cap that reaches them**:
scope down the side (personal, each team, the organization) and window across
the top.

In each window, **the smallest hard limit is the one that binds**. It is the
one highlighted cell in the grid, and the sentence underneath says it in words,
for example: *"No personal monthly cap, so Platform's limit of $5,000.00 is
what stops them."*

The grid updates as you type, before you save. Use that. Raising somebody's
monthly cap often reveals that a daily limit on their team now binds first.

Only hard limits count here. A budget with only a soft limit stops nobody.

## Find the accounts nothing stops

At the top of **People & teams**, a strip of flags counts accounts that need
attention. Click a flag to filter the list to exactly those accounts:

- **accounts have no cap above them**: no hard limit on the person, on any of
  their teams, or on the organization.
- **accounts have never used the gateway**
- **people on no team**: their spend rolls up to the organization only.

The roster's **What stops them** column gives the same answer per person:
*Personal cap*, *Team budget*, *Organization* or *No cap*. Panorama never says
"unlimited". A limit nobody set reads **not set**.

## What an engineer sees

**Past a soft limit**, on a route that keeps serving, nothing changes for them.
The response carries two headers, which clients ignore and you can log:
`x-panorama-budget-state: over_soft` and `x-panorama-budget-warning`.

**Past a soft limit**, on a route that stops, they get a refusal naming the
routes still served. See
[Routes](/docs/configure/routes/#decide-what-happens-past-a-soft-limit).

**Past a hard limit**, every request is refused with:

```
Panorama stopped this request: the monthly team budget is used up.

Your team has used $5,000.00 of the monthly $5,000.00 limit.
The limit resets at 2026-10-01 00:00 UTC (in 11 days).
```

There is no way to exempt one request or one person from a hard limit. Raise
the limit.

Both also appear on **Analytics → Attention**, with a link back to the budget.

## Good to know

- **Windows are UTC.** A day is a UTC day, a week starts on Monday, a month is a
  calendar month. Nothing carries forward.
- **A limit can be overshot slightly.** A request is priced when it finishes, so
  a limit is checked against spend as of the last completed request. Several
  long requests already in flight can take a budget a little past its line.
  This is the price of never touching the database on the request path.
- **A refused request costs nothing.** A request that was blocked or refused
  never counts against the budget that blocked it.
- **Unpriced requests are not in the figure.** If a model has no published
  rate, its requests are counted separately and the refusal message says the
  real figure is higher.
- **Deleting a team leaves its budget behind**, visibly, for you to remove.
  Deleting a team is refused while a service token bills it.
- **A service token's spend counts against its team.**

## Budgets as a file

```yaml
budgets:
  - scope: team
    id: platform
    period: month
    soft_limit: 4000.00
    hard_limit: 5000.00

  - scope: org
    period: month
    name: deployment
    soft_limit: 800.00
    hard_limit: 1000.00
```

`scope` is `org`, `team` or `user`. `id` is a team slug or a person's email,
and is left out for `org`. `period` is `day`, `week` or `month`. Apply it with
`panorama policy apply`, and read the caution about
[a policy file being the whole policy](/docs/configure/routes/#routes-as-a-file)
first. See the [Policy file reference](/docs/reference/policy/).

## Budgets are not cost centers

A budget limits what a **person or team** may spend, and it blocks. A
[cost center](/docs/configure/cost-centers/) reports what a **product or
project** cost, and it never blocks.
